LINANGKIT

ARCHIVAL TEXTILE ANALYSIS:
traditional decorative embroidery technique practiced by the Kadazan-Dusun community in Sabah.
The word linangkit comes from the local term meaning "to join" or "to connect," referring to the way colorful threads are intricately stitched together to form beautiful geometric patterns.
